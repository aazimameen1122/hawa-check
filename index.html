About this data

Source

Estimates come from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) model, delivered through the free Open-Meteo Air Quality API. They are modelled for an area, not measured by a sensor, so they can differ from a local reading. The model grid is coarse, which is why Srinagar and Pampore show identical values.

Reading the index

The US AQI combines PM2.5, PM10, ozone and other pollutants, so it is not set by PM2.5 alone. The guidance is general information, not medical advice. If you have asthma or another breathing condition, follow your doctor's advice first.
